How to pay your invoice

Business accounts receive one invoice per month covering all tickets booked by your team. Invoices are issued on the 3rd and are due within 30 days.

Paying by PayPal

Every invoice email includes a Pay now button. Click it, sign in to PayPal and pay the full balance. You can pay by PayPal balance, bank account or card. Please pay invoices in full; we do not accept partial payments.

Reminder: November invoices must be paid by 3 December to keep your corporate rate. Accounts more than 30 days overdue are moved to pay-as-you-go pricing.

Remittance and queries

Send remittance advice or questions about a charge to accounts@trainline.test and quote your invoice number. We never ask you to pay a different account by email. If you receive a message asking you to change payment details, do not pay it and forward it to security@trainline.test.

Need help?

Our virtual assistant can answer most billing questions instantly, or you can call the business desk on 0333 202 3390, 8am to 6pm weekdays.
